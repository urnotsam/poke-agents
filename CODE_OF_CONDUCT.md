# Code of conduct

This is a small project about putting Pokémon on your desktop. The bar is
simply: be decent to people.

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about a terminal behaving
  differently on someone else's machine, not about anyone being wrong.
- Critique code, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments. That includes about
  someone's setup, their experience level, or their choice of terminal.
- If you are asked to stop something, stop.

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove contributions and comments that do not
follow this, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, open an issue if it is safe to do so publicly, or contact
the maintainer through their GitHub profile if it is not.
